
# Kirby Star Allies Manual

## About Manuals
See the [standard manual guide](en_GeneralManualGuide_ManualTeam.md) for more information.

## How does Manual Kirby Star Allies work?
You'll start with a set number of stages and copy abilities to choose from while all the rest are randomized throughout the multiworld. You'll need to complete each stage as they come, collecting and accomplishing whatever you can with the limited Copy Abilities and Dream Friends at your disposal. 

### Basic Restrictions: 
Aside from the stages in your inventory, the following areas are accessible at any time throughout the run:

 - Ability Planet
 - Every Dream Palace
 - Dimension V's lobby (if Heroes in Another Dimension is enabled)

Meanwhile, the following areas, abilities, and features are locked behind certain items in the item pool:

 - All other Copy Abilities and Dream Friends
 - All other Story Mode Stages
 - All other Dimensions (depends on gating methods in the options)
 - Friend Actions
 - Every level of the Ultimate Choice
 - Every variant of Void Termina
 - Soul Melter EX
 - Every HAL Room
 - The secret route in Planet Caverna
 - The ambush gauntlet at the top of Ability Planet
 - Dimension V's final bosses

### Key Collectibles:
In addition to stages and allies, there are various other important collectibles necessary to reach the goal. These include:

 - **Heart Spear Piece**: Essential for every goal. Gathering enough will complete one of the four legendary Heart Spears (done solely in the Manual Client), granting access to a Friend Action. Completing all four Heart Spears is required to assemble the Star Allies Sparkler at The Divine Terminus AND access the final altar in Dimension V.
 - **Friend Heart**: Collecting enough of these is required to befriend the Three Mage-Sisters in *Heroes in Another Dimension* and achieve the Happiest Curtain Call. Depending on your options, they may also be how you unlock Copy Abilities and Dream Friends.
 - **Ultimate Spice Level UP**: Collecting one unlocks a new level of *The Ultimate Choice*. At least 9 are required to access every level, but some challenges have additional requirements.

### Other Items:

 - **Trip Ticket No. 86**: Allows access to each of the three HAL rooms.
 - **Caverna Back Road Pass**: Allows access to the secret harder route in Planet Caverna.
 - **Ability Planet Gauntlet Pass**: Allows access to the Ability Planet ambush gauntlet.
 - **Extraordinary Components**: Collecting all three will allow you to input the combination needed to access Soul Melter EX.
 - **Star Allies Sparkler**: As the ultimate Friend Action, it's required to fight any variant of Void Termina. It's obtained by completing every Heart Spear and activating the final pedestal at The Divine Terminus

## What are each of the goals?

 - **Kirby Star Allies - Final Episode Attack**: Defeat Void Termina in the final level of Story Mode. Requires completing the Star Allies Sparkler.
 - **Final Dimension - Happiest Curtain Call**: Befriend the Mage-Sisters and achieve a happy ending in Heroes in Another Dimension. Requires Friend Hearts and completing all four Heart Spears
 - **Soul Melter - Clear**: Defeat Void Soul at the end of Soul Melter. Requires completing the Star Allies Sparkler and collecting at least 8 Ultimate Spice Levels.
 - **Soul Melter EX - Clear**: Defeat the Astral Birth at the end of Soul Melter EX. Requires completing the Star Allies Sparkler, collecting 9 Ultimate Spice Levels, and obtaining the 3 Extraordinary Components.

## What do the locations look like?

Items in the manual are randomized among the following:

### Locations:

 - Stage Clears
 - Rare Picture Pieces
 - Big Switches

### Optional Locations:

 - Common Picture Pieces
 - 1-Ups
 - Blue Point Stars
 - Friend Hearts (Heroes in Another Dimension)
 - Dimension Clears
 - Ultimate Choice Boss Victories

### Manual Client Exclusive:

These can be unlocked in the client as soon as you meet the requirements:

 - Heart Spear Completion (gather enough Heart Spear Pieces)
 - Ally Unlock List (if enabled, gather enough Friend Hearts to recruit each ally)

## What does the location logic expect from me?
It's expected you have a moderate understanding of which abilities can accomplish what tasks. This may include some obscure interactions, like knowing Icicle Lance can cut burning chains, or that some of Vividria's paintings can pound stakes. Depending on how limited your ally selection is, some multiplayer intervention may be required to circumvent the AI's decision-making. 

For the time being, elements exclusive to the water's surface (i.e. Splash from Ninja and Blizzard from Animal Friends) aren't factored in.

I'm considering delegating some of these to a "hard logic" option, and I'd like to make a full interaction/location guide at some point if I ever find the time for it, but until then, assume you may have to look a few things up if you're unsure.

## How do the trap items work?
 - Remove Ally: Unfriend the specified ally from your team (first, last, any, or all). Swallowing them for their ability doesn't count.
 - Remove Copy Ability: Get rid of your copy ability (ideally ejecting it and spitting it out as a star bullet so it can't be made into an ally).
 - Sleep: Don't do anything for 5-10 seconds (about how long the Sleep ability usually lasts). You're free to simulate the ally wake-up action if you have any with you, but it's probably more fun by itself.
 - No Jumps: You can no longer jump, hover, fly, or take any related action with the A button (or whichever relevant button corresponds to your controller) for the rest of the stage. Attacks that increase your height are completely fine. Recommended you don't include too many of these, or at least ensure you can easily access an ability that grants height, like Sword or Whip.
 - Flip Controller: Play the rest of the stage with your controller flipped upsidedown. There are multiple ways to interpret this. Go with whichever you think is silliest.
### Recommended Rules for Traps:
 - Any of the above traps can be substituted with simply exiting the level. Useful if you really want to preserve your current team.
 - All ally/ability removal traps won't go through during moments where it'd be impossible. This includes Friend Actions, The Ultimate Choice, and Heroes in Another Dimension (in the case of abilities).
 - In general, it may be wise to not count ANY traps that'd devistate a long portion of gameplay, like "Sleep" in a particularly close run of Soul Melter EX, or "No Jumps" just before you reach Dimension II's Friend Train. They're meant to be fun challenges, not tedious roadblocks. If only allowing them during Story Mode makes the most sense for your playstyle, then do that.

## So what are all these filler items for?
They're mostly for fun. By default, only the ones directly related to amiibo are enabled, but the options have a wide range of different item names to use as you see fit. Maybe you just want more variety in the filler items you recieve. Maybe you want some special food item to counter a particularly devistating trap. Maybe you have a shop or some other bonus item system setup among your server that you'd like to represent in a fun way. However you decide to count these is entirely up to you and the server host.
